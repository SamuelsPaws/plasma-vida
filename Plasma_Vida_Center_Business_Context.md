## 3. Core Services

Plasma Vida Center offers three main service categories.

### 3.1. Sueroterapia — Intravenous Therapy

Intravenous therapy is one of the business's primary wellness services.

Plasma Vida Center offers vitamin-based IV therapies and describes its offerings as including homeopathic and vitamin-enriched formulations.

The business markets these treatments around general wellness, nutritional support, energy, and recovery.

The website includes a catalog where visitors can explore the available IV therapy products and formulations.

**Service characteristics:**

- Intravenous administration of fluids and/or other ingredients.
- Vitamin-based formulations.
- Multiple products and treatment options.
- A wellness-oriented approach.
- Personalized attention.

**Website catalog:** https://plasmavidacenter.com/catalogo

**Medical communication note:** The composition, indications, contraindications, and clinical evidence for each IV formulation should be confirmed individually. Marketing descriptions should not imply guaranteed improvements in immunity, energy, recovery, or health without appropriate supporting evidence.

The term "homeopático" appears in the existing business copy and requires clarification regarding which specific products it describes.

### 3.2. Plasma Rico en Plaquetas (PRP)

Platelet-rich plasma (PRP) therapy is another primary service category.

PRP involves preparing a platelet-enriched component from a patient's own blood and applying it in a treatment-specific context.

Plasma Vida Center presents PRP as a treatment associated with tissue repair, skin quality, and regenerative or aesthetic applications.

**Service characteristics:**

- Uses a preparation derived from the patient's own blood.
- Includes treatment-specific applications.
- Is presented as part of the business's wellness and regenerative treatment offering.

**Website catalog:** https://plasmavidacenter.com/catalogo#productos

**Medical communication note:** The specific PRP procedures offered, their intended uses, eligibility criteria, and expected outcomes must be confirmed before creating detailed service descriptions.

Claims about tissue regeneration, accelerated recovery, and skin rejuvenation should be specific to the procedure and supported by appropriate clinical evidence.

### 3.3. Servicios de Enfermería — Nursing Services

Plasma Vida Center offers professional nursing services focused on practical patient care and individualized assistance.

The business emphasizes a human, supportive approach that considers both the patient and their family.

Its nursing services include:

**Older adult care**

Nursing assistance and support for older adults who require additional care, supervision, or help with their daily health needs.

**Care for people with disabilities**

Individualized nursing assistance for people with disabilities who require professional care or additional support.

**Preoperative and postoperative care**

Nursing assistance before and after hospital procedures, according to the patient's condition and care requirements.

**General service characteristics:**

- Professional nursing attention.
- Personalized patient care.
- Assistance for patients with different care needs.
- Support for patients and their families.
- Home-based nursing services.

The business markets these services around patient comfort, well-being, safety, and peace of mind for families.

## 6. Brand and Communication

### Brand personality

Based on the business's current website copy, the intended communication style combines:

- Professionalism.
- Warmth and approachability.
- Personalized attention.
- Reassurance and trust.
- A focus on patient needs.
- An accessible approach to healthcare and wellness information.

The brand should feel professional and welcoming without becoming excessively clinical, impersonal, or promotional.

### Writing guidelines

Use clear, accessible Spanish appropriate for an Ecuadorian audience.

Explain healthcare services in language that prospective patients and their families can understand without requiring specialized medical knowledge.

Prioritize practical information about the services, their intended purposes, the care process, and what patients can expect.

Use reassuring, human language while remaining accurate about medical procedures and outcomes.

Avoid exaggerated wellness claims, guaranteed results, unsupported promises, or language that implies a treatment is appropriate for everyone.

Distinguish general wellness information from medical indications and recommendations.

For nursing services, emphasize the patient's individual needs, dignity, and the importance of professional care.

### Medical accuracy

The website should present health-related information responsibly.

In particular:

- Do not guarantee treatment outcomes.
- Do not claim that IV therapies automatically improve immunity, energy, or overall health.
- Do not describe PRP as universally effective for tissue regeneration, recovery, or rejuvenation.
- Do not imply that receiving IV therapy is inherently better than obtaining nutrients through ordinary nutrition.
- Do not invent medical certifications, professional qualifications, clinical evidence, or safety guarantees.
- Do not invent treatment ingredients, dosages, contraindications, or medical indications.
- Do not present homeopathic formulations as having clinically demonstrated benefits without appropriate evidence.

Specific medical claims, procedural descriptions, and treatment instructions should be validated against reliable clinical information and the provider's actual practices.
